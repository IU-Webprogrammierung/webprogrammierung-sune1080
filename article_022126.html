Welcome to the Investigation

21.02.2026

Devlog #1. I've been sitting on this project for a while now, mostly quietly, and it feels like the right time to finally start talking about it in public. If you're reading this, you're early — there's no trailer yet, no gameplay footage, nothing polished enough to show off.

What I can tell you is the shape of it: you play an FBI agent sent out to investigate a house, and by the time you're done, you'll understand why nobody wanted to take this call. I'm not going to spoil what's actually going on here — half the point of this game is not knowing, and I want that feeling to survive all the way to release.

Right now, the core concept and the first chapter are fully scoped out, environment work on the crime scene exterior is underway, and the found-footage sequence is in early prototyping. There's no release date yet — this is genuinely early days.

What I will do, in these devlogs, is talk about the process: the systems I'm building, the mistakes I'm making, the stuff that took ten times longer than I expected. If you're into game dev as much as the game itself, you're in the right place. First real update coming soon.
