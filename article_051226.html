Getting the Light Right

12.05.2026

One hallway, one red light at the end of it, and an unreasonable number of hours spent getting the glow right. This update was almost entirely lighting work — tuning reflection probes, adjusting how far the light bleeds down the hallway, and making sure it reads as unsettling rather than just "red for the sake of red."

HDRP's reflection probes turned out to be a bigger headache than expected — an early version had bright, blown-out reflections bouncing off the hallway floor that looked more like a nightclub than a crime scene. Rebuilding the probe setup and dialing back the intensity fixed most of it, though I'm still tweaking the falloff so the light feels like it's actually coming from somewhere specific, not just filling the whole space evenly.

This particular hallway shows up more than once over the course of the game, in more than one context. I'll leave it at that.
