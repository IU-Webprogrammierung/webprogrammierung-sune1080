A Different Kind of Night

16.04.2026

Not everything in this game happens during the day, and not everything happens from the same perspective. This update was environment work on a nighttime forest sequence that plays a different role in the story than the crime scene itself — I'll leave it at that for now.

Getting a convincing "lit only by moonlight and a rotating patrol light" look took longer than expected. Static lighting looked flat and staged; the trick ended up being animated light sources moving through the trees, throwing shifting shadows rather than a fixed, obviously-placed spotlight. Small technical detail, big difference in how alive the forest feels.

This location connects to something the player encounters much later, from an angle I'm not ready to talk about yet. For now: dark woods, moving lights, and the sense that you're not entirely alone out there.
