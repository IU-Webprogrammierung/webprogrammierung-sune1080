Sealing Off the Scene

05.04.2026

Every investigation starts somewhere, and for this one, it starts with a strip of tape across a forest path. This update was about getting that first moment right — the point where the player crosses from "just walking through the woods" to "this is now a crime scene."

It's a small space, but a surprisingly important one. Too clean and orderly, and it doesn't feel like an active scene; too cluttered, and it stops reading clearly. I went through a few passes on the tape itself, the surrounding markers, and how the fog sits along that stretch of road before landing on something that felt right.

There's also a lighting pass in here I'm not going to say much about yet, tied to how this location looks at a very specific point later in the game. You'll notice it eventually. For now, this is just the entrance — quiet, sealed off, and waiting to be searched.
