The website serves as a hub for a video game that I am currently developing.
Visitors should be able to get a first impression of the game through a description, screenshots, and videos.
Regular news posts also provide insight into the development process,
supplemented by a newsletter that allows interested parties to stay up to date.
In addition, users should be able to quickly and easily access the platforms on which the game will be available. Related links to the associated social media channels will also be included. An “About” subpage also provides a brief insight into who I am and the history of the game’s creation.
