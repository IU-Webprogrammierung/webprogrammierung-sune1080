Chapter 1 Is Playable

13.05.2026

This is the update I've been building toward since devlog #1: Chapter 1 is playable from start to finish. Every system from the last few months — the environment, the found-footage sequence, the ambience work, the interaction system, the character work — is now in place and working together as one continuous experience, not a collection of separate tech demos.

I'm still not going to tell you what happens in it. That's not me being coy for the sake of marketing — I genuinely think this game works best when you go in with as little as possible spoiled for you, and I'd rather protect that experience than get a few extra clicks out of a devlog.

For transparency: nothing in Chapter 1 is final yet. A few interactions are still a little rough around the edges, one or two textures are placeholder-obvious if you go looking for them, and there's some pacing and balancing left to do. But the full loop works, start to finish — and playing through it myself, alone, later at night than I probably should have, was a mistake I don't recommend repeating.

Thank you to everyone who's been reading along since the very first devlog. For a solo project like this, that kind of attention means more than I can really put into words.

Next up is Chapter 1.1 — a shorter chapter, connected to something the player finds at the very end of Chapter 1. I'm not going to say anything more specific than that. It's the chapter I've been most excited to build out of everything so far, and I think it'll make a lot of things click into place once it's out. See you in the next one.
