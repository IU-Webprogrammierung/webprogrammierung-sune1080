STEAM ITCH.IO

What is this game about?

A foggy morning. A stretch of forest road, sealed off with crime tape. You're an FBI agent, sent to investigate a house at the end of the path — and something about the silence tells you this isn't a routine call. The property outside gives nothing away. But the house does. Something happened here, and it wasn't quiet. Upstairs, the evidence is impossible to ignore — and impossible to look away from. It didn't happen tonight, though. A recording recovered at the scene pulls you back to the night it started: an officer, sent out alone, chasing reports of strange sounds and stranger lights coming from a house buried in the woods. Moonlight and the flicker of his own patrol lights are the only things guiding him down a steep, overgrown slope, toward a window glowing an unnatural red. Someone (or something?) is watching him from between the trees. He goes inside anyway. The door slams shut behind him — and won't open again. What he found in that house is still waiting for you to find it too. Piece together two crime scenes from two different nights, one clue at a time — the notes left behind, the things caught on camera, the details no one was meant to see. Chapter by chapter, the house starts giving up its secrets. Whether you'll like what they are is another question.
