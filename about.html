About

Sune Roch

Solo Developer

Hi, my name is Sune, and I am a solo game developer. I have been working on this game for a while now, and I am excited to share it with you. I started this project because I always dreamed of making a game entirely by myself, from start to finish. It's a lot of work for one person, but it's also the part I enjoy most about doing this. I love atmospheric and deeply immersive games that pull you into their world, and I wanted to create something that would do the same. This game has been shaped by a lot of the games and stories I love myself, and I hope that comes through when you play it. I hope you will enjoy playing it as much as I enjoyed making it.
