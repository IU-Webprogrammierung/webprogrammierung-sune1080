What's Behind Every Door

02.05.2026

This update stayed inside the house. After spending previous updates on the exterior and the road leading in, it was time to actually build out the rooms the player walks through once they're inside — hallways, doorways, and one basement door in particular that took more passes than I'd like to admit.

The challenge with interiors like this is restraint. It's tempting to fill every room with detail, but a house that's too busy stops feeling real and starts feeling like a prop set. I ended up removing more furniture and clutter than I added, and the emptier version reads far better — quiet, a little too tidy, like whoever lived here didn't have much left to disturb.

I'm not going to say what's behind that particular door yet. Some things are better left for the player to open themselves.
