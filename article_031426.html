Building the Road Into the Woods

14.03.2026

This update was almost entirely environment work on the very first thing a player sees: the forest road leading up to the crime scene. Turned out to be a much harder mood to nail than expected — too dense with trees and it reads as a generic hiking trail, too sparse and open and it stops feeling like anywhere a crime scene would plausibly exist.

I ended up rebuilding the tree placement along the approach road three separate times, and tuned the fog density more times than I want to admit — an earlier version looked more like a spooky forest movie set than an actual overcast morning. I also spent an entire evening on how the police tape moves in the wind, because a completely static piece of tape was somehow breaking the whole atmosphere on its own.

Small details, disproportionate amount of time spent on them. That already seems to be the running theme of this project. Blocking out the property surrounding the house has also started, so the next update should have a bit more to show.
